Scope and payment

Deliverables, timelines, source files and third-party expenses are agreed before work starts. Standard payment is 50% upfront and 50% before final handover. Confirm cancellation and refund terms in the project agreement.

Standalone revisions

Two consolidated rounds of in-scope feedback are included. Rounds three through five each cost 15% of the relevant service price. Further revisions and new scope require a separate quote.

Complete Brand Identity

The Brand Deck is developed and approved before implementation. Significant changes after approval cost 20% of the full ₹15,000 package (₹3,000). Major changes requiring extensive rework cost 30% (₹4,500). Joel explains the fee and timing impact and obtains approval before starting additional work.

Timeline and delivery

A standalone service normally takes about seven days, with about three extra days for each additional service. Complete Brand Identity takes about 21 days. Timelines depend on receiving materials and feedback. Final assets are handed over after the outstanding payment.
